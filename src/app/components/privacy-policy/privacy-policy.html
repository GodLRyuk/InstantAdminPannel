INSTANT DELIVERY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Instant Delivery Admin application (the “App”), used by administrators and restaurant owners to manage their stores, handles information. The App is intended for business users and is not designed for children.

Information we handle

  • Account information: email address and password submitted when you sign in, and account details such as username, user ID, and role.
  • Business and operational information: product and inventory records, orders and delivery or customer details included in those orders, store and restaurant details, staff accounts, sales, settlements, expenses, schedules, promotions, and reports that authorized users enter or view.
  • Content you provide: product, category, banner, or other images and files uploaded through the App, and questions or other text submitted to the analytics assistant.
  • Technical and device information: the App stores access and refresh authentication tokens and basic account state in your browser’s local storage. Our hosting and API providers may receive standard connection information such as IP address, browser/device type, and request time in server logs. The App does not request precise device location or access contacts, camera, or microphone.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, enforce account permissions, operate store management features, process and display business records, generate reports and analytics, provide the analytics assistant when used, maintain security, troubleshoot failures, and support the service.

How information is shared

The App sends sign-in information and the business data needed for requested features to the Instant Delivery API. Information may be processed by the service’s hosting, storage, and infrastructure providers so they can operate and secure the service. When you use the analytics assistant, your prompt and the relevant information sent with it are processed by the analytics service and any providers configured by the service operator. Authorized administrators and restaurant users may see information according to their roles. We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own advertising.

Retention and security

Account and business records are retained by the service operator while needed to provide the service and meet operational or legal requirements. Retention of server backups and logs depends on the operator’s hosting configuration. Authentication tokens remain in this browser until you sign out, clear site data, or they expire. The App uses HTTPS for its production API connection. No online service can guarantee absolute security; protect your sign-in details and use a trusted device.

Your choices and requests

You can sign out to remove the App’s locally stored session, and you can clear this site’s browser storage to remove locally stored tokens. To request access, correction, or deletion of account or business information, contact the administrator or organization that provided your account. The organization operating your account controls the business records managed through the App.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the App or its data practices change. The current version will be available at this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact the organization that provided your Instant Delivery account.
